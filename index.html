FOFUS — Data Deletion Instructions

FOFUS apps collect the minimum Facebook data needed to provide the service, and you can delete it at any time. Here's how:

Option 1 — Delete via Facebook (recommended)

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and Websites.
  3. Select the FOFUS app and click Remove.
  4. Choose "Delete your activity and connections" when prompted.

Facebook then notifies us, and all records tied to your app-scoped ID are deleted within 30 days, per the Meta Platform Terms.

Option 2 — Request deletion directly

  1. Email support@fofus.in with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include the email or username you used with the app.
  3. We confirm deletion by reply within 30 days.

What data we hold

  • Your app-scoped user ID (a random ID that never identifies you outside the app)
  • Your public profile name, if you granted access
  • Any content you created while using the app

We never post to your profile, never sell data, and never use your information for advertising. Deletion is permanent once processed.
